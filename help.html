Help and contact

Answers about orders, delivery, returns, warranty and paying monthly.

How do I know my order went through?

You see an order number on screen straight away and receive an email confirmation. You can follow it on the Track order page.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, until it leaves the warehouse. Call or WhatsApp us with your order number.

Do you sell to businesses?

Yes. For 5 or more units, contact us for a business quote and VAT invoice.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is free on orders over Rs 5,000. Below that it costs Rs 300 anywhere on the main island.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 24 to 72 hours. We call the day before with a time slot.

Do you deliver to Rodrigues?

Yes, by sea freight. We send you a quote before shipping.

What is Click & Collect?

Order online, choose a showroom, and collect your order there. Most orders are ready within 2 hours.

What is your return policy?

Unopened items can be returned within 7 days with the receipt for a full refund.

My product arrived damaged. What do I do?

Do not sign the delivery note as received in good condition. Take photos and contact us the same day and we replace it.

Who handles warranty repairs?

Our service desk in Port Louis. Bring the product with your receipt, or ask us to collect large appliances.

How long is the warranty?

It depends on the product and is listed on every product page. Most electronics have 1 or 2 years.

Who can apply?

Anyone over 18 living in Mauritius with a regular income.

What documents do I need?

Your National Identity Card, your latest payslip or bank statement, and a utility bill as proof of address.

How fast is approval?

Most applications are answered within one working day.

Is there a deposit?

No deposit is needed on plans up to 24 months. 36 month plans may need a 10% deposit.
